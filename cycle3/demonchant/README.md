## Description

Current is a Nimiq Pay Mini App for people who want to supply USDT to or withdraw it from Aave V3 on Polygon. It shows the live market rate and wallet position, and checks the transaction receipt and updated position before confirming completion.

## Builder story

I built Current from a problem I know well: wanting my savings to grow, but feeling unsure how to put them to work. Money sitting still can feel like money losing ground, yet earning shouldn’t mean handing over control or signing things you don’t understand. I created Current to make that choice easier to see and review: check Aave’s live USDT rate, understand each step, and verify what happened on Polygon. Your funds stay in your wallet until you approve a transaction, and the rate can change there are no guaranteed returns.
